# Contributing

Corrections should identify the affected file, the exact publisher-PDF page, and the distinction between a source claim and a proposed interpretation. Preserve Jorge Garza Ulloa's sole-author credit and the article's DOI, dates, and page range.

Keep the guide's explanations original. Do not add the PDF, a full-text extraction, or copied figures. Keep hypothetical examples visibly labeled and update metadata when changing a claim.

Do not promote the seven pillars, the intake-time illustration, or the secondary demand statistic into validated findings. If adding evidence from another work, read that work, register it as a new source, and state its population, method, limits, and relationship to this framework.

Run `python scripts/validate_repository.py` before submitting changes. Documentation checks cannot validate research conclusions. Verify new external URLs separately and record substantive provenance changes in metadata/paper.json.
